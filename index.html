About the numbers above: when the selected video source exposes an exact byte size, the download percentage is calculated as downloaded bytes ÷ total bytes and labeled “exact”. When only an estimate is available it is labeled “estimated”, and when no size information exists at all it shows “unknown” rather than a misleading number. Download coverage (green) is separate from what your browser has actually buffered (grey) and from your current playback position (blue).
